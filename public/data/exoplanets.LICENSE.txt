exoplanets.json は Open Exoplanet Catalogue を変換したデータです。
This data is derived from the Open Exoplanet Catalogue, https://github.com/OpenExoplanetCatalogue/open_exoplanet_catalogue.git (commit 30e582f1f6eed54e57182de40bc5a428f575a3ca 2026-11-04).
Changes: kept only confirmed planets and the fields listed in "fields"; matched host stars to the HYG-based stars.bin.
